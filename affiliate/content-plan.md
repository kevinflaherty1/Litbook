## Rules for every AI draft
- Facts (prices, laws, specs) are checked against a primary source and linked.
- Licensing/insurance articles say "check your state/city" and link official
  sources. No legal advice.
- Price guides give ranges and cite sources, and say so where a number is an estimate.
- Software reviews describe only what you clicked through in the trial, with
  your screenshots. Anything else is labeled "per the vendor's site".
- Affiliate disclosure at the top; links use `rel="sponsored nofollow"`.
