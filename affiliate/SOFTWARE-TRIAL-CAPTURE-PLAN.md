# Software Trial Capture Plan: Jobber, Housecall Pro, QuoteIQ

The goal is honest, first-hand reviews that Google trusts and readers believe. You run
the **same test job in each app**, take the **same screenshots**, and jot quick notes.
I turn that into reviews and a comparison.

**Time:** about 60–90 minutes per app, split across two or three sittings if you like.
Do one app at a time.

---

## Before you start (once)

- [ ] **Sign up through the normal website**, never through an affiliate link (including
      ours). Self-referrals break program rules.
- [ ] Use **hello@soapandsqueegee.com** and the business name **Soap & Squeegee Test**.
- [ ] Note the **trial length** and whether a **credit card is required**. Set a phone
      reminder 2 days before the trial ends so you can cancel.
- [ ] Have a **second email address** (your personal one) to play "the customer".
- [ ] **Don't enter real customers' data.** Use the test job below.

## The test job (use it in every app)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Customer | **Test Customer** (your personal email as the customer email) |
| Address | Any real-looking public address in your town (e.g. a park or library) |
| Line 1 | Driveway cleaning, ~500 sq ft: **$150** |
| Line 2 | House soft wash, vinyl, ~2,000 sq ft: **$450** |
| Optional add-on | Oil stain treatment: **$40** (mark as optional if the app allows) |
| Recurring job | Storefront window cleaning, **every 2 weeks**, $45/visit |

Same job everywhere = a fair comparison.

## What to capture (same list for each app)

Name screenshots like `jobber-01-pricing.png`, `hcp-01-pricing.png`,
`quoteiq-01-pricing.png`, using the step numbers below. If renaming is a hassle on your
phone, just send them in order with the step number in your message.

Use a stopwatch for the steps marked ⏱.

### 01. Pricing page (before signing up)
- [ ] Screenshot the full **pricing page**, every plan.
- [ ] Note: cheapest plan price, what's missing from it, monthly vs yearly price,
      any per-user fees.
- [ ] Note today's date (prices change; we'll say "as of [date]").

### 02. Sign-up and first screen
- [ ] Screenshot the **first dashboard** you land on.
- [ ] Notes: credit card required? Trial length? How many setup questions? ⏱ minutes
      from sign-up to usable dashboard.

### 03. Services / price book
- [ ] Add "Driveway cleaning" and "House soft wash" as saved services.
- [ ] Screenshot the **service list**.
- [ ] Note: can you price per sq ft, or only flat prices?

### 04. Add the customer
- [ ] Add Test Customer. Screenshot the **customer record**.

### 05. Build the quote ⏱
- [ ] Create the quote with both lines plus the optional add-on.
- [ ] Screenshot the **quote editor**.
- [ ] Note: ⏱ minutes to build it, could you mark the add-on optional, add photos,
      add your terms?

### 06. What the customer sees
- [ ] Send the quote to your personal email (and by text, if offered).
- [ ] Screenshot the **email/text the customer gets** and the **quote page or PDF**.
- [ ] As "the customer", **approve** it (and tick the optional add-on if possible).
      Screenshot the **approval screen**.
- [ ] Note: did it look professional? Could the customer pay a deposit?

### 07. Turn it into a job and schedule it
- [ ] Convert the approved quote into a job and schedule it. Screenshot the
      **calendar/schedule view**.
- [ ] Note: ⏱ how many taps from approved quote to scheduled job?

### 08. Recurring job (window cleaning route)
- [ ] Create the storefront job **every 2 weeks**. Screenshot the **recurring settings**
      and how it looks on the calendar.
- [ ] Note: easy or awkward? Can it auto-invoice each visit?

### 09. Mobile app
- [ ] Install the phone app and log in. Screenshot the **home screen**, the **job
      view**, and **creating a quote on the phone**.
- [ ] Note: could you comfortably quote standing in a driveway? Anything only on desktop?

### 10. Invoice and payment
- [ ] Create the invoice from the job and send it to your personal email.
- [ ] Screenshot the **invoice** and the **customer payment page**. **Don't pay with a
      real card** unless the app has a clearly marked test mode.
- [ ] Note the **card processing fee** if shown, and payment options (card, ACH, tap
      to pay).

### 11. Automations
- [ ] Look for quote follow-ups, appointment reminders, review requests and "rebook"
      reminders. Screenshot the **automation/settings page**.
- [ ] Note which are included in the cheapest plan vs locked behind upgrades.

### 12. Reports
- [ ] Screenshot the **main report/dashboard** (revenue, jobs, quotes).

### 13. Support
- [ ] Ask support one real question via chat or email, for example *"Can I price
      driveways per square foot?"*
- [ ] Screenshot the reply. Note ⏱ how long it took to answer and whether it helped.

### 14. Cancellation
- [ ] When finished, cancel the trial. Screenshot the **cancel flow**.
- [ ] Note: easy, or did they make it hard? Any data export option?

## Privacy before sending screenshots
- Crop or scribble out any **real** personal info (your home address, personal phone).
  The test customer data is fine to show.
- Don't screenshot payment card numbers or bank details.

## How to send them to me
