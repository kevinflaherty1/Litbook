# SaaS Affiliate Option — Research (Nov 2026)

## SaaS categories checked

| Category | Example programs | Payout (reported) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Home-service business software** | Housecall Pro, Jobber, QuoteIQ | HCP: up to $320/qualified lead + $180/enrollment (PartnerStack). Jobber: 20% recurring or $50–300/sale (PartnerStack). QuoteIQ: 40% recurring for life | **Best fit** |
| Freelancer/creative client management | HoneyBook, Dubsado | HoneyBook: $50 after 100 days. Dubsado: mostly account credit | Payouts too small |
| Salon/spa booking | Vagaro, Fresha, GlossGenius | Mostly platform credit, not cash; Fresha closed to new affiliates | Skip |
| Horizontal tools (CRM, email, hosting, project mgmt) | HubSpot, etc. | 25–35% recurring | Most competitive niche for affiliates; skip |

## Recommended angle
**"Start and run a small home-service business."** Pick 1–2 trades first:
pressure washing, window cleaning, lawn care, or residential cleaning.

- **Traffic articles (AI drafts these well, from public facts):** how to start a
  pressure washing business, what to charge, licenses and insurance by state,
  how to get your first 10 clients, quoting templates.
- **Money articles:** Jobber vs Housecall Pro for solo operators, best quoting
  app for window cleaners, QuoteIQ review. Built from **real free-trial
  walkthroughs**: you click through each app for 30 minutes and take
  screenshots. That's honest, first-hand use at $0 cost.
- **Interactive tools (I can build these):** a pricing calculator ("what to
  charge per sq ft"), a quote template generator, a startup-cost calculator.
  These earn links and are hard for AI-only sites to copy.

## Why it can beat the garden plan for you
- **$0 product cost.** Free trials replace buying hardware.
- **Fewer sales needed.** About 5 software signups a month (~$500–1,500) equals
  roughly 60–100 garden sales at ~$15 each.
- **Suits AI + pen name.** B2B readers care about accurate how-to info and
  screenshots more than a personal face.
- **Buyers are motivated.** Someone starting a business needs this software now.

## Risks (honest)
- The vendors' own blogs (Jobber, Housecall Pro) rank strongly for "how to start
  a ___ business". We go narrower: specific trades, specific questions, tools.
- Google's AI Overviews answer simple how-to questions, so calculators,
  templates and screenshots matter more than plain text.
- Programs approve applications by hand. Have 10–15 solid articles live first.
- Payouts depend on the referred customer staying subscribed (e.g. qualified
  leads, retention periods). Read each program's terms.
