# Traffic Plan: SoapAndSqueegee.com

Built for **under 5 hours a week** and **$0–$100/month**. Ranked by how much each
channel is worth for this site.

## Who we're trying to reach
People **starting or running** a small pressure washing or window cleaning business.
They hang out in a few specific places, search Google with very specific questions,
and love free tools (price calculators, estimate templates).

---

## 1. Google search (main channel, slow but compounding)
- **Months 1–3:** Google is still learning the site. Expect little traffic. Keep publishing.
- **From week 4–6:** open Search Console → **Performance → Search results** and look at
  **Queries**. Questions we already show up for (even at position 20–50) tell us what
  to write next or which article to expand. Send me a screenshot monthly.
- **Keep the target narrow:** specific questions ("how much to charge to clean a
  2-car driveway") rank faster than broad ones ("pressure washing business").
- **Refresh** pricing guides every year. Updated dates help.

## 2. Bing, ChatGPT search and other AI answers (free, 2 minutes)
- Bing Webmaster Tools is already imported from Search Console. Bing powers several
  AI search tools.
- **Turn on Cloudflare Crawler Hints:** domain → **Caching → Configuration →
  Crawler Hints → On**. Cloudflare then pings Bing and other engines (via IndexNow)
  whenever pages change, so new articles get noticed in days.
- AI assistants like to cite **specific numbers and tools**, which is exactly what our
  calculators provide.

## 3. Communities (fastest early traffic, about 2 hours a week)
Where new operators ask questions every day:
- **Reddit:** r/pressurewashing, r/windowcleaning, r/sweatystartup, r/smallbusiness
- **Facebook groups:** search "pressure washing business" and "window cleaning
  business". Several have tens of thousands of members.
- **Trade forums:** PressureWashingResource (PWR) and WindowCleaner.com forums

**How to do it without getting banned:**
1. **Answer questions fully in the post itself.** Give the actual numbers and advice.
2. Link to the site only when it really helps (for example "here's a free calculator
   I made for exactly this") and **say it's your site**. Honesty here is required by
   most community rules and builds trust.
3. **Read each community's rules first.** Many limit self-promotion to roughly 1 in 10
   posts or to specific threads.
4. Never post the same link everywhere, and never use affiliate links in communities.
5. Use a consistent account name (e.g. "Cole from Soap & Squeegee").

The calculators and estimate template are the best things to share. People bookmark
and pass along free tools.

## 4. Backlinks (links from other sites make Google trust us)
- **Tools earn links naturally.** Mention the calculators when helping people.
- **Journalist requests:** sign up free at **Qwoted** or **Featured.com** and answer
  questions about small business, home services or pricing. Published answers often
  link back.
- **Ask suppliers and local business resources** (chamber of commerce startup pages,
  SBDC resource lists) if they'd list a free calculator.
- **Never buy links.** Google penalizes it.

## 5. Email list (later, once traffic exists)
Offer a free **"Pricing Cheat Sheet"** or **"Startup Checklist"** PDF in exchange for an
email. Subscribers come back for every new review, which is where affiliate income comes
from. Free plans from email tools cover the first 500–1,000 subscribers. I can build
this when we're getting ~1,000 visits a month.

## 6. Video (optional, highest effort)
Before/after pressure washing videos do very well on YouTube Shorts, TikTok and
Instagram, but they need real footage. Skip unless you start doing jobs yourself.
**Screen recordings of the software trials**, though, can become short YouTube review
videos that link to the written reviews.

## 7. Paid ads: not yet
Google Ads for "pressure washing business" terms are expensive, and affiliate programs
often ban bidding on brand names. Revisit only once we know which pages earn money.

## Realistic expectations
| Time | Typical for a new niche site |
|---|---|
| Months 1–3 | Mostly community traffic; tens to low hundreds of visits/month |
| Months 4–6 | Search traffic starts; a few hundred to ~1,000 visits/month |
| Months 6–12 | Compounding search traffic if we keep publishing; first affiliate commissions |

Software referrals pay $50–$300+ each, so even modest, well-targeted traffic can earn.
