# Startup Budget & 12-Month Model

## Startup costs (lean)
| Item | Low | Typical |
|---|---|---|
| Domain (.com, yearly) | $10 | $15 |
| Hosting (yearly) | $0 (static/Vercel) | $60–180 |
| Theme / design | $0 | $60 |
| Keyword tool (monthly, optional) | $0 | $30–130 |
| Email platform (free tier to start) | $0 | $0–20/mo |
| Products to review (often resold/returned) | $0 | $200–500 |
| LLC formation (optional, varies by state) | $0 | $50–500 |
| **First-year total** | **~$10** | **~$600–2,000** |

## Revenue model
Revenue = Visits × CTR × Conversion × Avg commission

| Month | Visits | CTR | Conv. | Avg comm. | Est. revenue |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 500 | 8% | 4% | $12 | $19 |
| 6 | 3,000 | 8% | 4% | $12 | $115 |
| 9 | 10,000 | 8% | 5% | $14 | $560 |
| 12 | 20,000 | 9% | 5% | $15 | $1,350 |

Illustrative only — swap in your own niche's numbers. Many sites grow slower;
some faster. Track actuals monthly and compare.
