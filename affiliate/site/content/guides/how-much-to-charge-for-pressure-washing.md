Most residential pressure washing is priced **per square foot**, with a **job minimum**
so small jobs still pay for your drive and setup time. Below are the typical ranges
published for 2026, how to use them, and the mistakes that make new operators underprice.

## Typical 2026 rates by surface

| Surface | Typical $/sq ft | Example job |
|---|---|---|
| Concrete: driveway, patio, sidewalk | $0.15–$0.35 | 2-car driveway (400–600 sq ft): roughly $100–$300 |
| Vinyl siding house wash (soft wash) | $0.12–$0.35 | 2,000 sq ft of siding: about $240–$700 |
| Stucco | $0.18–$0.40 | Higher than vinyl: more absorbent, slower to clean |
| Wood or composite deck | $0.30–$0.60 | 300 sq ft deck: about $90–$180, often higher with stain removal |
| Wood fence | $0.30–$0.55 | Some contractors price fences per linear foot instead |
| Vinyl fence | $0.20–$0.40 | |
| Roof soft wash | $0.30–$0.75 | Highest risk and chemical cost, so price at the top for steep roofs |

For whole jobs, many homeowners pay roughly **$150–$500**, and a two-story house wash
commonly lands around **$300–$500**.

## Job minimums

Set a minimum charge and stick to it. Published minimums commonly run **$150–$350**.
Without one, a "quick" $60 patio job can cost you money once you count driving, setup,
cleanup and fuel.

## Add-ons that raise the ticket

- **Oil stain treatment:** about $25–$75 per stain
- **Concrete sealing:** about $0.50–$1.00 per sq ft
- **Gutter brightening, window rinsing, fence or deck add-ons** when you're already on site

Offering two or three add-ons on every quote is the easiest way to raise your average job.

## How to adjust for your market

1. **Check local competitors.** Look up three local companies' prices or ask for quotes.
   Rates vary a lot by region and cost of living.
2. **Adjust for condition.** Heavy algae, years of grime or deep stains take longer. Our
   calculator adds 15% for moderate and 30% for heavy buildup. That's our own rule of thumb,
   so adjust it to your experience.
3. **Adjust for access and risk.** Second and third stories, steep roofs, delicate
   surfaces and tight access belong at the top of the range or above it.
4. **Check your hourly rate.** Estimate how many hours a job takes. Hourly rates for
   pressure washing are commonly quoted around $50–$100 per hour for basic work
   ([AirQuote](https://airquote.co/blog/pressure-washing-rates-2026)). Established
   operators often target well above that, around $150–$300 per hour of on-site work,
   once they have efficient equipment.

## Common pricing mistakes

- **Pricing only by the hour as a beginner.** You're slow at first, so per-square-foot
  pricing protects you, and you earn more as you get faster.
- **No minimum.** Small jobs eat your day.
- **Forgetting chemicals and fuel.** Soft wash solution, surfactants and fuel add up.
- **Guessing square footage.** Measure, or use satellite views to estimate before you quote.
- **Never raising prices.** Review your rates every year.
